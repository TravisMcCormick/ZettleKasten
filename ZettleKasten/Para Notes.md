#Organization 
### **P — Projects**

**Short-term efforts with a clear outcome and deadline.**
- Has a finish line
- Actively worked on
- Drives tasks and notes

**Examples**
- Migrate firewall rules to new platform
- Write Q1 security assessment report
- Deploy SIEM upgrade

If it ends, it’s a project. No debate.

---
### **A — Areas**
**Ongoing responsibilities with no end date.**
- Must be maintained    
- Measured by standards, not completion

**Examples**
- Security operations
- Personal finances
- Health
- Team management

Areas generate projects when standards slip.

---
### **R — Resources**
**Reference material you may need again.**
- No obligation to act
- Supports projects and areas

**Examples**
- Vendor documentation
- Threat intelligence notes
- Cooking recipes
- Research articles

If it’s “interesting” but not actionable, it belongs here.

---
### **A — Archive**
**Inactive or completed material.**
- Finished projects
- Obsolete areas
- Old resources

Nothing here should demand attention.

Archive aggressively.
